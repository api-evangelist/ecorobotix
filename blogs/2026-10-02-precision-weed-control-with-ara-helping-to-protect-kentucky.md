10/02/26 Precision Weed Control with ARA™ Helping to Protect Kentucky Bluegrass Seed Quality Grower experience highlights how precision weed control can help deliver cleaner seed, while Oregon State University results demonstrate 92% targeting accuracy and reduced herbicide use of just 2.9 gal/acre. For Kentucky bluegrass seed growers, weed control is about more than protecting the crop in the field. Weeds such as annual bluegrass (Poa annua) and downy brome can contaminate harvested seed, create additional cleaning costs and reduce the value of the final crop.
